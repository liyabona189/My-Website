UniBites

Student Food Ordering System

About the Project

Uni Bites is a proposed web-based food ordering system designed for university students. The system will allow students to view available meals, place orders, and manage their orders conveniently.

Project Objectives

  • Make food ordering easier for students.
  • Allow students to view available meals and prices.
  • Allow students to place and manage orders.
  • Provide a simple and user-friendly system.

Main Features

  • Student registration and login
  • Food menu
  • Food ordering
  • Order management
  • Order confirmation
